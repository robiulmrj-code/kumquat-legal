Kumquat Demo

Privacy Policy

For the Kumquat Demo and Kumquat Rider Demo apps on Google Play. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What these apps are

Kumquat Demo and Kumquat Rider Demo are portfolio demonstrations of a food-delivery system. The restaurants, menus, riders and customers in them are fictional. Orders, payments and deliveries are simulated: no food is delivered, no money is taken, and no card details are ever asked for.

The apps are made and run by an independent developer, the "developer" below. Contact: mrrobiul.mrj+kumquat@gmail.com.

Demo sessions

When you tap Try the demo, the app creates a demo session on our server. A demo session has no email, password or name. It is identified only by a random ID. The server gives it a fictional name, phone number and address so you can place a simulated order straight away.

Every demo session, and everything linked to it, is deleted automatically 7 days after it starts. You can delete it sooner at any time (see Deleting your data).

What is stored, and why

Only what the apps need to work. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone for their own use.

  • Session ID: to keep you signed in and keep your demo data separate from everyone else's.
  • Anything you type in: a changed display name or phone number, delivery addresses, delivery instructions and support messages. The apps work without any real details, and we ask you not to enter them.
  • Simulated orders and deliveries: what was ordered, simulated prices and status history, so the apps can show progress and history.
  • Rider app, location: only if you allow it, and only while you are carrying a sample delivery, the app sends your phone's precise position so the delivery screen can track it. It is never collected in the background, and the Customer app never reads your location. You can use Simulate route instead and never share a real position. Positions are deleted with the session.
  • On your phone only: your cart, whether you have seen the introduction, and the sign-in session. These are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. They are excluded from Android backup.

The apps contain no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and do not use the advertising ID.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts the server, database and sign-in (servers in Seoul, South Korea). It processes data only on the developer's instructions.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation serves the map tiles on the Customer app's order tracking screen. Your phone requests the tiles directly, so the OpenStreetMap servers see your IP address and the map area shown, under the OSMF Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these web pages.
  • Google Play distributes the apps under Google's own privacy policy.

Security

All traffic between the apps and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules make each demo session's data readable only by that session. A demo rider sees a sample delivery's fictional address only while carrying it. Server keys never ship in the apps.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile, then Delete my account. The session and its personal data are deleted at once.
  • Automatically: 7 days after the session started.
  • On the web: see Delete your account, or email the developer with the Session ID shown on your Profile.

After deletion, simulated order records may remain only without any name, phone number or street address, so the demo restaurants' figures still add up.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and contact

You can ask what data a session holds, have it corrected or deleted, or raise a concern, by emailing mrrobiul.mrj+kumquat@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
