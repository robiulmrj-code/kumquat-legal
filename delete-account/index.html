Used "Explore Kumquat"?

A guest session has no email or password, so it cannot sign in on this page. Its data is fictional apart from anything you typed in. To delete it:

  • In the app, open Profile, then Delete my account. It is deleted at once.
  • Or do nothing: every guest session and its data are deleted automatically 7 days after it started.
  • Or email support with the Session ID shown on your Profile, and it is deleted within 7 days.

What happens

  • Your name, phone number, sign-in, saved addresses, favorites, notifications and vehicle details are deleted.
  • Past orders, payments, deliveries and earnings are kept for accounting, without your name, phone number or street address.
  • Reviews you wrote stay, without your name.
  • If an order or delivery is still in progress, you can delete your account once it is finished.
  • Restaurant and administrator accounts are closed by our team: contact support.
