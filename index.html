Kumquat Demo

A food-delivery system you can try

Two Android apps from a portfolio project: a customer app and a rider app, sharing one backend with a restaurant dashboard and an admin console.

Kumquat Demo (customer)

Browse fictional restaurants, build an order, check out with simulated cash or a test card, and watch a simulated rider bring it on a live map. About three minutes from order to delivery.

Kumquat Rider Demo

Ask for a sample delivery, accept it, pick it up, follow a simulated route and confirm the cash. Everything works on one phone, with no waiting.

Simulated by design

No email is needed: tap Try the demo. No food is delivered, nothing is charged, and every demo session is deleted after 7 days.
